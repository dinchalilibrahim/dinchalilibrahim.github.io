## Topic: QR Codes (Quick Response Codes)

Welcome to my presentation page. My presentation focuses on how QR codes store data in a two-dimensional matrix and how they are read by modern devices.

### Key Highlights
- **Two-Dimensional Data:** Storing text, URLs, and data vertically and horizontally.
- **Error Correction:** Reed-Solomon error correction algorithm to read damaged codes.
- **Everyday Applications:** Mobile payments, ticketing, and digital menus.
